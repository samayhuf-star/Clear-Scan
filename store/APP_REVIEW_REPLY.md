Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the feedback on guideline 4.3(a). We have made substantial changes so that ClearScan is a distinct app and not a repackaged scanner. The new build includes functionality and design not found in typical scanner apps:

1. Facts: after a scan, ClearScan reads the text on the device and extracts structured details: expiry and due dates, totals and amounts, emails, phone numbers, links, and ID numbers (card, PAN, Aadhaar, IBAN). They appear in a dedicated "Facts" tab and can be copied with one tap.

2. Deadline reminders: on any expiry, renewal or due date the user can tap a bell to schedule local notifications (7 days before, 1 day before, and on the day). A new "Deadlines" screen lists every upcoming date across all scans. This turns scans of passports, insurance, warranties and bills into a personal deadline tracker. Reminders are local; nothing is uploaded.

3. Share-safe text: a "Share text, hide sensitive numbers" action masks card, Aadhaar, PAN and IBAN numbers (keeping the last four digits) before text is shared.

4. New identity: a new app icon, colour system and onboarding copy built around this "find the dates that matter" concept. The app is developed by a single developer and is not built from a template or shared with other accounts.

All processing remains on the device. There is no account, no server and no tracking. The paywall is optional: scanning, OCR, search, Facts and reminders are free. ClearScan Plus unlocks extra export formats and larger limits.

To try it: scan any document that contains a date (for example a bill with a "Due date"), open it, choose the "Facts" tab, and tap the bell next to the date. Then open the calendar icon on the home screen to see it in "Deadlines".

Thank you for your time and for reviewing the updated build.

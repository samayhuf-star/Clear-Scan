## Review notes
No account, login, or demo account is required.

Keptly is a document scanner. Scanning, on-device text recognition, search, PDF export, and Face ID app lock are free and have no watermark. Scans stay on the device.

How to reach the paywall:
1. Skip or finish the introduction.
2. Open Settings and tap Get Keptly Plus. Or, on the home screen, tap a type filter (Receipt, Invoice, ID, or Letter). Or open a document, tap More, and choose Export as Word (Plus) or Export as Markdown (Plus).
3. The paywall lists Auto-sort by type and Word and Markdown export. It shows the monthly and yearly price and billing period, the 7-day free trial on those subscriptions, and the lifetime one-time price. Restore Purchases, Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy are on the paywall. Close with the X or Not now. Free features stay available.

Purchases, US prices (the app shows the App Store's localized price):

- com.samayhuf.clearscan.pro.lifetime — $39.99 one-time, no trial, does not renew

Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. The in-app name is Keptly Plus. Those three App Store products unlock it.

## Description
Scan anything. Keep it private. Pay nothing for the basics.

ClearScan turns your iPhone into a fast, clean document scanner. Every scan is read on your device, so you can search the words inside it. No watermark. No account. No ads.

FREE, ALWAYS
• Unlimited scans with automatic edge detection
• Text recognition (OCR) on your iPhone, no internet needed
• Search names and the text inside every scan
• Export searchable PDF, plain text or images
• Choose PDF size: small, medium or original
• Lock the app with Face ID
• Import photos from your library

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Nothing is uploaded. Ever.
• No sign-up, no tracking, no ads

CLEARSCAN PLUS (optional)
• Auto-sort scans into Receipts, Invoices, IDs and Letters
• Export as Word or Markdown
Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage in your Apple ID settings.

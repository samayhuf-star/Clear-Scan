ClearScan scans documents and reads their text entirely on your device.

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No uploads. Scans, extracted text and PDFs stay in the app's storage on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • No tracking. ClearScan has no ads, analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Purchases. If you buy ClearScan Pro, Apple processes the payment and subscription status through StoreKit on your device. We do not use a third-party purchase SDK. Apple never receives your scans from us.
  • Camera and photos. Used only to scan or import documents you choose.
  • Face ID. Optional app lock handled by iOS; we never see biometric data.
  • Sharing. Files leave the app only when you tap Share.

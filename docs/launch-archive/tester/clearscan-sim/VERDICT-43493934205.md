## Why 01 / 02 / 04 were missing

Evidence: `flow-error.txt`, `maestro/02_import.log`, `maestro/03_export.log`, `maestro/04_home.log`, `maestro/05_paywall_sandbox.log`, `screenshots/05-paywall-sandbox-failed.png`.

1. **02-scanning.** After "Choose photos", Maestro's 8 s wait for `scanning-screen` warned (not found). The QA hold is also 8 s, so the wait can expire as the overlay comes up. The flow then took the "picker not visible" branch and tapped 20%,30% on whatever was on screen (the overlay or home), and never took `02-scanning`. The scan itself succeeded (03-result is the QA fixture). This is not a missing home screen or a crash. An earlier run (43427632722) did see `scanning-screen`; the shot was lost to a later race, which is why the hold was lengthened to 8 s in the first place.

2. **04-export.** `03_export.yaml` tapped `doc-export-pdf` (completed) then asserted visible "PDF size" (failed, 8 s). Share PDF opens `ActionSheetIOS` with title "PDF size" and rows Small / Medium / Original. On the iOS 26 simulator the sheet title is often absent from the accessibility tree, so Maestro never confirmed the sheet and never took `04-export`. The sheet was left up.

3. **01-home and sandbox `home-paywall`.** Official `01-home` is taken in `04_back_home.yaml` after returning to a list that includes the new scan. With the export sheet still up, `doc-back` completed but did not leave the document screen; optional "Cancel" was not found (same AX issue). `home-screen` was not visible. The later sandbox paywall then failed to find `home-paywall` because it was still on the document: `screenshots/05-paywall-sandbox-failed.png` is the same invoice result, not a missing paywall control. `home-paywall` exists on home (`src/app/index.tsx`) and was used successfully by the XCUITest after a fresh launch.
